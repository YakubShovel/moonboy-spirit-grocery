How much is too much? When does pleasure turn to pain?

Something I notice about my attention is that the more it splits the more it splits. Like being passed under a knife into a finer and finer mince. The more I expel it, the greater the urge to expel more. There is no enough. I just have to close the tap.

Close the tap.

Just close the tap.

Do not keep spending. Do not keep scratching the itch. There is no peace beneath the scab. Just. Stop. Stop and let the heat rise, the volume turn up, cacophony. Until it just stops. It is just the hunger that is hungry. It is the boredom that is bored. Just let it be. Just be. It will pass. And then I can begin to get full again.
