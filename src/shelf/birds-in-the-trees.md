I hear the birds in the trees
In this world made of speech
I can hear the Lord whisper

Divine frequencies, harmonics and seraphim, and
The Ophanim singing, choir of His children

Breeze blowing, senses keep flowing
I don't end at the skin
I'm you and I'm Him
Something about sin, I'm

Meditating on that moment that you hold it in
World narrows to the point of pin
Animal need is start and end

Till you let it out, relief, moment, then a draught
Like what's next? I need a fix
Is it all a test?

Ah there's a woman
What if I could have it all?
Curly headed footsteps patter in the hall

As the vision clarifies there's more to lose
Try to shake it off, try to play it cool

Try to trust in Hashem, here now and here then
He will guide my steps if I let Him
Trust Him and don't settle

Sigh, give back His breath
His depth
What's next?
